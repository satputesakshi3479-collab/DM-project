Daily Beauty Routine ✨

A simple and easy routine to help you organize your everyday self-care habits.

Keep Your Routine Simple

A good routine does not need to be complicated. Start with a few basic habits and follow them consistently. Your routine can be adjusted according to your personal needs.

01

Clean

Start your routine by gently cleaning your face to remove dirt and excess oil.

  • Use a gentle cleanser
  • Avoid harsh scrubbing
  • Rinse with comfortable-temperature water
02

Care

Give your skin the care it needs by keeping your routine simple.

  • Choose products suitable for you
  • Introduce new products carefully
  • Keep your routine consistent
03

Moisturize

Moisturizer can help support the skin's natural barrier and reduce dryness.

  • Apply after cleansing
  • Choose a suitable moisturizer
  • Use a comfortable amount
04

Protect

During the daytime, protect exposed skin from excessive sun exposure.

  • Use sun protection
  • Seek shade when appropriate
  • Consider protective clothing
